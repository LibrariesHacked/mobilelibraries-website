# Feature Inventory

## Service

- Browse and filter library services by local authority.
- Search UK places and postcodes to find nearby services and stops.

## Mobile

- Browse mobile libraries currently on the road and the rest of the fleet.
- View a vehicle's live or estimated location, status, and stop progress.
- See the routes served by a selected vehicle.

## Route

- Filter stops by route and view route lines and labels on the map.
- Select a route to focus the map on its stops.

## Stop

- Browse and locate stops on the map, with optional distance-based results.
- Expand stop details to view address, schedule, and access information.
- Download calendar files, print stop flyers, and follow timetable links.
